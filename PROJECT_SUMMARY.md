# Task 4 — Location-Based Analysis (Project Summary)

## Objective
Perform a geographical analysis of restaurants using their latitude and longitude and compare restaurant density, ratings, cuisines, and price ranges across cities and localities.

## Methodology
1. Load the restaurant CSV dataset using Pandas.
2. Convert geographic and numeric columns to numeric types and handle missing values.
3. Filter the data by city, locality, rating, and price range.
4. Plot valid latitude/longitude records on an interactive map.
5. Aggregate records by city and locality to calculate restaurant counts, mean ratings, average costs, and price-range statistics.
6. Split the comma-separated cuisine field to identify common cuisines.
7. Display charts and tables and allow CSV exports.

## Tools
Python, Pandas, Streamlit, Plotly, VS Code, GitHub.

## Expected outcome
A dashboard that makes it easier to identify restaurant clusters, compare ratings between areas, understand cuisine availability, and examine price distributions. Actual findings should be reported after running the app and inspecting the selected dataset results.
